# CONFIRM_LATER

Where you can land guesses which aren't pivotal for later confirmation or adjustment.


## Markdown highlighting, 📜 and the 🧾 indent

* A line broken to fit ends every style, as a newline does, and its
  repeated quote and indent are plain.
* Every ```` ```m8 ```` example in README and docs now has its raw
  markdown form directly under it, for you to pick between.
  * Converted mechanically: ▐/│ to ####/>, and a sigil after either to
    its shortcode.
  * Both forms of every example parse under the new grammar.
